## Unit, outcome and chronological boundaries

- Unit: retailer-SKU (StockCode) at daily granularity; there is one retailer and no store identifier. Forecast gross positive purchased units, excluding returns/cancellations and non-product charge codes. This is observed sales, not uncensored latent demand.
- Horizon: **28 consecutive daily steps from a fixed origin**. No future actual demand enters any step within a horizon. Evaluation has two non-overlapping validation origins; retraining for the second origin may use the first window's observations because they are then historical.
- Initial training coverage: 2009-12-01 through **2011-09-15**, inclusive. Validation origin 1: 2011-09-15; targets 2011-09-16 through 2011-10-13. Validation origin 2: 2011-10-13; targets 2011-10-14 through 2011-11-10, using expanding history through that origin.
- Final test origin: **2011-11-10**; targets **2011-11-11 through 2011-12-08**. Final incomplete December 9 is excluded by a predeclared calendar rule. The final test labels are sealed separately and are not used for EDA, features/hyperparameters, SKU selection or winner selection.
- Minimum history: 365 daily observations from first positive transaction to initial training origin, at least 60 positive-sales days in initial training, and valid positive observed price. No observed sale on a complete day is encoded as zero recorded sales after first observation, explicitly assuming a complete transaction export; missing/invalid source dates are never automatically zero.
- Study subset: 60 eligible SKUs, deterministically stratified across three initial-training zero-demand-fraction groups, sampled with seed 20261001. If a group has fewer than 20, fill from remaining eligible SKUs using the same seeded order. Export exact included SKUs, initial-history statistics and every exclusion reason before training. This is a bounded case study, not a claim of full-dataset or Kenyan performance.

## Quality, preprocessing and holdout protection

Verify workbook sheets/columns/counts/dates/types and preserve raw hashes. Structural source auditing can count invalid/missing/duplicate records; demand/outlier/price/trend/seasonality analysis and subset selection use initial training only. Never drop a SKU based on validation or test error. Remove exact duplicate transaction records consistently and report counts; repeated non-identical invoice lines are preserved. Exclude cancellation invoices, nonpositive/invalid quantity or price, invalid dates/identifiers, and explicit non-product codes (stock code must be five digits with zero to two trailing uppercase letters). Source customer ID is excluded from features and exported data. Keep missing descriptions/customer IDs where core transaction fields are valid. Report overlapping filter counts and exclusive rejection reasons clearly.

Daily quantity sums positive transactions; daily observed price is the quantity-weighted transaction price. Forward fill price from past observations within each SKU only; never backfill or use future prices. No target outlier clipping: retain and report extreme purchases and weak product forecasts. No category, store, promotion or stockout labels are inferred from product descriptions.

## Features and models

Features: SKU one-hot columns, demand lags 1/7/14/28, prior-only rolling mean/std 7/28 (shift 1), calendar day-of-week/month/day-of-year sine/cosine/weekend, previous known price, and past price change over 7 days. Missing warm-up feature rows are excluded from training with recorded counts. During a fixed-origin forecast, lag/rolling demand uses only prior history plus earlier model predictions; future price is held at the last observed origin price. Future known calendar features are allowed; actual future observed prices or demand are prohibited.

Baselines: seasonal naive (repeat last observed week) and historical 28-day mean. Models: global Random Forest Regressor and XGBoost Regressor (CPU). No neural networks. Seed 20261001, two CPU threads per estimator. Predeclared RF configurations: 80 trees/depth 12/min leaf 5 and 80 trees/depth 12/min leaf 15. XGBoost configurations: 200 trees/depth 3/learning rate 0.05/min child weight 10 and 150 trees/depth 5/learning rate 0.05/min child weight 10, histogram tree method, squared-error objective, subsample/column sample 1, regularization lambda 5. Exact library versions are pinned in ml/requirements.txt. Nonnegative predictions are enforced; actual labels are never clipped. Default unlisted estimator parameters are captured from the fitted configuration.

All candidates forecast the same SKU/date/origin/horizon rows. Rank by pooled validation **MAE**, then RMSE, then deterministic candidate name for ties. Choose best RF and best XGBoost configuration on validation only; select the overall winner including baselines. A baseline can win. Write an immutable frozen-selection record with parameters, feature specification, data/protocol hashes, observation keys and timestamp **before** opening test labels. Refit frozen family configurations on all history through 2011-11-10, then evaluate every final candidate on identical test observations. No test-driven parameter/subset changes. Save predictions, overall/per-SKU metrics, the worst products and representative training-selected SKUs.

MAE and RMSE in units/day are primary metrics. MAPE is omitted because intermittent/zero-demand days make full-sample percentages undefined and a positive-only subset would change coverage and interpretation. Record zero proportions; do not manufacture a percentage by adding epsilon.
