The repository's development CSV is a changed/derived daily aggregate: filtering, duplicate handling, training-only cohort selection, purchased-unit aggregation and prior price filling are described in docs/research. It contains date, product StockCode, aggregate demand and price, not customer/invoice identifiers. Preserve attribution, license and change notices when sharing derived data. Raw workbook/customer data is not committed.

The tiny frozen baseline artifact and documented research results are included to reproduce the historical demonstration without retraining. The original dataset, protocol, source fingerprints and model results remain unchanged. UK2009–2011data does not validate current Kenyan forecasting accuracy.
